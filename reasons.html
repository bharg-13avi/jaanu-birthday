Why You're Special 💕

I could probably write a hundred reasons...
but honestly, some people are just impossible to describe in a few words. 🌸
Dear Jaanu, 💖

I don't think I can explain everything that makes you special in just a few sentences. There are so many little things about you that I appreciate, and most of them are things you probably don't even realize. 🌸

Thank you for being my best friend.
And thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my life. 🌸

I hope our friendship stays crazy, beautiful, and unforgettable forever. 🥹❤️

Always stay the amazing person you are. 💕
